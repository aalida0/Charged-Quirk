# Scans of decaying charged quirks (patched QuirkPheno, LO, generic CMS-like detector)

Two parts.

1. **Lambda x c*tau grid at M = 500 GeV** (`make_sample.sh`, `scan.sh`, `point.sh`, `analyze_scan.py`, `plot_scan.py`; data in `data/scan_triplet_M500.csv`, `data/benchmark_*_M500.csv`). Drell-Yan Q+Q- pairs (sigma = 3.1 fb in the generated process, 3806 of 4000 events with |eta| < 2.5), triplet decay table throughout the grid, Lambda = 10, 30, 100, 300, 1000, 3000 eV, c*tau = 1, 5, 20, 54, 200, 1000, 5000 mm, **2000 charged quirks at every point** (the Lambda = 3 keV, c*tau = 5 m point was re-run with 1000 events). Same 1000 generator events at every point. Columns: see `analyze_scan.py` (`f_tracker`, `f_nl_ge3`, `dke_median`, ...). `helix_scan_M500.csv` (`analyze_helix.py`, `plot_helix.py`, `helix_fraction_M500.png`): helical-compatible fraction of the tracks with >= 4 tracker hits; solid = circle-fit RMS < 0.1 mm on unsmeared hits, dashed = `analyze_helix_smeared.py` / `helix_smeared_M500.csv` (hits smeared by 10 um pixel / 30 um strip, geometric circle fit, chi2/ndf < 3 and R > 0.5 m; with 4 hits ndf = 1, so the cut alone costs ~8% at Lambda <= 30 eV).

2. **Mass scan** (`massscan/`, outputs in `data/massscan/`): M = 300, 500, 750, 1000, 1500, 2000 GeV, doublet (T = 1/2, Y = 1/2, kappa = 1) and triplet (T = 1, Y = 0, kappa = 2) with the finite-M one-loop Delta m and the decay table of each case (`massscan_params.txt`: doublet Delta m 314 -> 349 MeV, c*tau 9.5 -> 6.6 mm; triplet 164 -> 167 MeV, 59.5 -> 54.2 mm), Lambda = 30, 100, 300, 1000 eV, 1000 events per point, both production modes.
   * `massscan/gen_mass.sh M nev`: Pythia samples (DY 17 17bar; W*-mediated 17 18 / 17bar 18bar via process 3021, off-shell W' at m_W with vector-like coupling) filtered to |eta| < 2.5; `generator_summary.txt` has sigma and the kept fractions.
   * `massscan/run_ms.sh`, `ana2.sh`: simulation grid and analysis (`analyze_ms.py`, `analyze_helix.py`); `summarize_ms.py` -> `massscan_summary.csv` (cross sections and yields in fb; multiply by the luminosity in fb^-1); `plot_ms.py` -> `massscan_{xsec,yield}.png`.
   * DY events are re-weighted event by event from Pythia's gamma*/Z coupling of a chiral 4th-generation lepton to the vector-like couplings of the multiplet member (`reweight_dy.py`: <w> = 1.8-2.0 doublet, 6.1-7.2 triplet; the Z coupling carries 1/(sw^2 cw^2)); the W* rate is multiplied by kappa_W^2 = 1 (doublet), 2 (triplet). The triplet numbers are multiplied by 2 charged Dirac species (a Dirac triplet = 2 Majorana winos), done in `summarize_ms.py` and `recast_hscp.py` (`SPECIES`).
   * Observables per point: charged-quirk decay in the tracker, terminating-track candidate (decay in the tracker after hits in >= 3 layers), neutral-pair-leaves-detector classification with the generator-level pair pT as MET proxy (`f_evt_neutralpair_*`, `f_evt_timeout` = a quirk is still alive at the 500 ns limit), helical-compatible fraction.
   * `recast_hscp.py` -> `data/massscan/hscp_recast.csv`: probability that a charged quirk reaches r = 116 / 129 / 420 cm before decaying (straight line, no string slowing) and the surviving cross section, for the predicted c*tau and for a c*tau scan.
   * Sensitivity runs at M = 500 GeV (`massscan/extra_runs.sh`, `data/sensitivity/`): (i) doublet decay table at the triplet c*tau and Delta m: terminating-track fractions agree within 4%; (ii) ITk-like layout (`massscan/itk_like.card`, radii typed from memory, NOT verified; B = 2 T): terminating-track fraction x 0.4-0.5 for Lambda <= 300 eV and x 0.08-0.36 at 1 keV, neutral-pair yield unchanged.

## Main numbers (LO, |eta| < 2.5, Pythia pSet 20; multiply by K ~ 1.25, see below)

* Production, Lambda-independent, M = 500 GeV: DY 5.3 (doublet) / 36 (triplet) fb; W* 18.6 / 74.5 fb. W*/DY = 3.5 (doublet), 2.1 (triplet) at 500 GeV.
* Triplet, decays in the tracker after >= 3 layers (Lambda = 100 eV): 123, 13.4, 1.03, 0.126, 6.0e-3, 5.1e-4 fb at M = 300 ... 2000 GeV. Doublet: 0.39 fb at 300 GeV, 4e-3 fb at 500, none above (c*tau = 7-10 mm).
* Neutral pair leaves after a decay (generator level, pT(pair) > 250 GeV): triplet 6.8 fb (500 GeV); doublet 1.45 fb; independent of Lambda. With the monojet-style selection (`jetmet_gen.py`, `monojet_yield.py`, `data/massscan/monojet_yield.csv`: anti-kT 0.4 jets, leading jet pT > 100 GeV, |eta| < 2.4, MET from visible particles > 250 (400) GeV, no dphi/jet-multiplicity/lepton veto, 100% trigger): triplet 41, 6.3, 1.2, 0.22, 0.021, 0.0022 fb (MET > 250) at M = 300 ... 2000 GeV; doublet 8.9, 1.3, 0.27, 0.048, 0.0047, 5e-4 fb.
* HSCP: P(charged quirk reaches the end of the tracker) = 8.8e-5 ... 2.2e-8 (triplet, M = 300 ... 2000 GeV), ~0 for the doublet; surviving triplet cross section 0.081 fb (300), 8.3e-4 (500), 8.6e-6 (750), 2.6e-7 fb (1 TeV).
* Helical-compatible fraction (>= 4 hits): ~1.00 for Lambda <= 30 eV (smeared: 0.93-0.99); at c*tau = 1 (5) m, unsmeared / smeared: 0.92/0.81 (0.76/0.60) at 100 eV, 0.49/0.28 (0.21/0.10) at 300 eV, 0.27/0.006 (0.17/0.002) at 1 keV.

## Recast limits (`recast_limits.py` -> `data/massscan/recast_limits.csv`)

No HEPData or CMS public pages could be fetched here, so no limit curves are used. Instead the published exclusion of a wino is used as a calibration point and the same flat-upper-limit approximation is applied to the Dirac triplet: a mass is excluded if sigma_triplet x eps_rel(Lambda, M) >= sigma_wino-eq(M_cal), eps_rel = terminating-track (decay-in-tracker) rate relative to the Lambda = 30 eV rate, sigma_wino-eq = sigma_triplet/2 (2 Majorana winos). Own LO cross sections are used on both sides so the K-factor cancels. Calibration points, all read from the papers' text/abstracts: ATLAS disappearing track 2201.02472, wino excluded to 660 GeV at 0.2 ns; CMS 2004.05153, wino 474 GeV at 0.2 ns (884 GeV at 3 ns); CMS HSCP 2410.09164, DY tau' (Q = 1e) excluded below 1.02 TeV (101 fb^-1).

* Dirac triplet (c*tau = 54-59 mm, a bit above the 0.2 ns point of the calibration): excluded to ~750-765 GeV (ATLAS calibration) and ~550-560 GeV (CMS calibration) for Lambda = 30-100 eV, ~735 / 535 GeV at 300 eV; ~365 GeV at 1 keV, but there eps_rel ~ 0.03 is outside the range where the calibration is meaningful and the helix criterion is almost certainly failed (see the smeared helix fraction), so treat it as 'no sensitivity'. The sensitivity of these numbers to MC noise in eps_rel is ~ +-15 GeV.
* Doublet: none (c*tau ~ 7-10 mm, decays inside the pixel detector). HSCP (flat tau' plateau, 0.066 fb, the most optimistic limit): none excluded; the surviving triplet cross section is 0.081 fb at 300 GeV, where the real limit is much weaker than the high-mass plateau, and < 1e-3 fb from 500 GeV.
* Limits are approximate: a flat UL ignores the mass dependence of the efficiency and of the published limit, ignores the different track-quality criteria (a non-helical track may fail the DT requirements: the smeared helix fraction would multiply eps_rel by 0.3-1 for Lambda = 100-300 eV), and ignores the lifetime reweighting of the calibration.

## Production normalisation (`wino_pythia_check.py`, `kfactor_check.py`)

* Independent LO check: Pythia's own SUSY processes (pure-wino SLHA spectrum, SUSY:qqbar2chi+chi-, qqbar2chi+-chi0, same PDF, 13 TeV) give sigma(chi+chi-) = 19.9 fb and sigma(chi+chi0 + chi-chi0) = 41.2 fb at 500 GeV. My per-wino totals (sigma_gen x <w> for DY, kappa_W^2 sigma_gen for W*) are 0.96 (DY) and 0.95 (W*) of those at 500 GeV, 0.96/0.99 at 300 GeV, 0.89/0.89 at 1-2 TeV (`data/massscan/kfactor_check.csv`). The DY/W* ratio of the SUSY processes is 0.48 at 500 GeV, as in my samples.
* Public NLO+NLL anchor: ATLAS 2106.01676 gives sigma(chi1+- chi2 0, wino) = 3.42 fb at 850 GeV. The Pythia-SUSY LO chi+-chi0 (both charges) is 3.03 fb at 850 GeV (log-interpolation between 750 and 1000), K = 1.13; against my own samples (about 0.9 of Pythia-SUSY LO at high mass) K ~ 1.25. Only this one public point was verified; K at other masses and for chi+chi- is assumed equal (NLO+NLL wino K-factors are mass dependent). The tables in `data/` stay at LO.

## Caveats

* Leading order; the W* and DY samples are Pythia templates (process 3021 with a vector-like W'; gamma*/Z with chiral 4th-generation lepton couplings re-weighted analytically). The re-weighting reproduces Pythia's SUSY wino processes to 4-11% (above), no electroweak corrections, Pythia internal PDF set 20 instead of LHAPDF NNPDF3.1.
* Generic detectors only: CMS-like (the card is a reconstruction of public dimensions, see `cards/README.md` upstream) and an ITk-like variant with unverified radii. Hits are sensitive-volume visits, not reconstructed tracks; the smeared helix fraction is a circle-fit proxy with a Gaussian smearing, no pattern recognition, no material effects, no rz fit. It is not a reconstruction efficiency.
* The neutral-pair monojet numbers are generator level with a simple selection: no trigger simulation (100% assumed), no jet energy resolution or MET resolution, no veto selection, and no recast of a published monojet analysis. For scale, the ATLAS 139 fb^-1 monojet model-independent limits quoted in arXiv:2108.02204 are sigma x A x eps < 736 fb for recoil > 200 GeV and < 0.3 fb for > 1200 GeV (not re-checked against the ATLAS paper), far above the 41 fb (MET > 250 GeV, M = 300) here: the monojet-like signature of the neutral pair is not constraining at these masses, and mostly useful as an HL-LHC-era or trigger-level handle.
* The per-event agreement of the handover test is limited by step discretisation (see `../quirkdecay/README.md`).
* The patched transport kills a quirk whose forced step ends outside the world; before the fix, Lambda = 10 and 30 eV, c*tau = 1 m runs segfaulted at event 530 of the 500 GeV DY sample. The underlying tiny hIoni step is not identified.
* Not done: limit curves from HEPData; two-loop splitting; a full reconstruction-level terminating-track efficiency; K-factors at every mass.
