# Scans of decaying charged quirks (patched QuirkPheno, LO, generic CMS-like detector)

Two parts.

1. **Lambda x c*tau grid at M = 500 GeV** (`make_sample.sh`, `scan.sh`, `point.sh`, `analyze_scan.py`, `plot_scan.py`; data in `data/scan_triplet_M500.csv`, `data/benchmark_*_M500.csv`). Drell-Yan Q+Q- pairs (sigma = 3.1 fb in the generated process, 3806 of 4000 events with |eta| < 2.5), triplet decay table throughout the grid, Lambda = 10, 30, 100, 300, 1000, 3000 eV, c*tau = 1, 5, 20, 54, 200, 1000, 5000 mm, **2000 charged quirks at every point** (the Lambda = 3 keV, c*tau = 5 m point was re-run with 1000 events). Same 1000 generator events at every point. Columns: see `analyze_scan.py` (`f_tracker`, `f_nl_ge3`, `dke_median`, ...). `helix_scan_M500.csv` (`analyze_helix.py`, `plot_helix.py`, `helix_fraction_M500.png`): helical-compatible fraction of the tracks with >= 4 tracker hits; solid = circle-fit RMS < 0.1 mm on unsmeared hits, dashed = `analyze_helix_smeared.py` / `helix_smeared_M500.csv` (hits smeared by 10 um pixel / 30 um strip, geometric circle fit, chi2/ndf < 3 and R > 0.5 m; with 4 hits ndf = 1, so the cut alone costs ~8% at Lambda <= 30 eV).

2. **Mass scan** (`massscan/`, outputs in `data/massscan/`): M = 300, 500, 750, 1000, 1500, 2000 GeV, doublet (T = 1/2, Y = 1/2, kappa = 1) and triplet (T = 1, Y = 0, kappa = 2) with the finite-M one-loop Delta m and the decay table of each case (`massscan_params.txt`: doublet Delta m 314 -> 349 MeV, c*tau 9.5 -> 6.6 mm; triplet 164 -> 167 MeV, 59.5 -> 54.2 mm), Lambda = 30, 100, 300, 1000 eV, 1000 events per point, both production modes.
   * `massscan/gen_mass.sh M nev`: Pythia samples (DY 17 17bar; W*-mediated 17 18 / 17bar 18bar via process 3021, off-shell W' at m_W with vector-like coupling) filtered to |eta| < 2.5; `generator_summary.txt` has sigma and the kept fractions.
   * `massscan/run_ms.sh`, `ana2.sh`: simulation grid and analysis (`analyze_ms.py`, `analyze_helix.py`); `summarize_ms.py` -> `massscan_summary.csv` (cross sections and yields in fb; multiply by the luminosity in fb^-1); `plot_ms.py` -> `massscan_{xsec,yield}.png`.
   * DY events are re-weighted event by event from Pythia's gamma*/Z coupling of a chiral 4th-generation lepton to the vector-like couplings of the multiplet member (`reweight_dy.py`: <w> = 1.8-2.0 doublet, 6.1-7.2 triplet; the Z coupling carries 1/(sw^2 cw^2)); the W* rate is multiplied by kappa_W^2 = 1 (doublet), 2 (triplet). The triplet numbers are multiplied by 2 charged Dirac species (a Dirac triplet = 2 Majorana winos), done in `summarize_ms.py` and `recast_hscp.py` (`SPECIES`).
   * Observables per point: charged-quirk decay in the tracker, terminating-track candidate (decay in the tracker after hits in >= 3 layers), neutral-pair-leaves-detector classification with the generator-level pair pT as MET proxy (`f_evt_neutralpair_*`, `f_evt_timeout` = a quirk is still alive at the 500 ns limit), helical-compatible fraction.
   * `recast_hscp.py` -> `data/massscan/hscp_recast.csv`: probability that a charged quirk reaches r = 116 / 129 / 420 cm before decaying (straight line, no string slowing) and the surviving cross section, for the predicted c*tau and for a c*tau scan.
   * Sensitivity runs at M = 500 GeV (`massscan/extra_runs.sh`, `data/sensitivity/`): (i) doublet decay table at the triplet c*tau and Delta m: terminating-track fractions agree within 4%; (ii) ITk-like layout (`massscan/itk_like.card`, B = 2 T; radii now checked against the public ITk layout of arXiv:2412.15090: pixel 34/99/160/228/291 mm, strip 399/562/762/1000 mm; the only change was one strip layer, 40.5 -> 39.9 cm; the DY point was re-run with the corrected card, `data/sensitivity/triplet_dy_M500_itk_verified_radii.csv`, terminating-track fractions 0.089/0.067/0.002 at 100/300/1000 eV against 0.085/0.064/0.001 before, i.e. unchanged within MC noise): terminating-track fraction x 0.4-0.5 for Lambda <= 300 eV and x 0.08-0.36 at 1 keV, neutral-pair yield unchanged. Layer radii and B are verified; module sizes, material budget and stereo geometry are still generic.

## Main numbers (LO, |eta| < 2.5, Pythia pSet 20; NLO+NLL scaling with the mass-dependent K of the table below)

* Production, Lambda-independent, M = 500 GeV: DY 5.3 (doublet) / 36 (triplet) fb; W* 18.6 / 74.5 fb. W*/DY = 3.5 (doublet), 2.1 (triplet) at 500 GeV.
* Triplet, decays in the tracker after >= 3 layers (Lambda = 100 eV): 123, 13.4, 1.03, 0.126, 6.0e-3, 5.1e-4 fb at M = 300 ... 2000 GeV. Doublet: 0.39 fb at 300 GeV, 4e-3 fb at 500, none above (c*tau = 7-10 mm).
* Neutral pair leaves after a decay (generator level, pT(pair) > 250 GeV): triplet 6.8 fb (500 GeV); doublet 1.45 fb; independent of Lambda. The earlier unmodelled monojet-style numbers (anti-kT 0.4, leading jet > 100 GeV, MET > 250 GeV, 100% trigger, no smearing) are superseded by the trigger- and resolution-modelled yield of `atlas_dt_recast.py` (below), which is NLO+NLL-scaled: pre-selection (ATLAS-SUSY-2018-19 MET trigger map, MET > 200 GeV, leading jet > 100 GeV, min dphi(jet, MET) > 1, jets and MET smeared) of the Dirac triplet, Lambda-independent, 60.0, 10.4, 1.86, 0.42, 0.030 fb at M = 300, 500, 750, 1000, 1500 GeV (`sigma_presel_NLO_fb` in `data/massscan/atlas_dt_recast_*.csv`; MC errors 2-3%). `monojet_yield.py` / `monojet_yield.csv` stay as the unmodelled upper-side reference.
* HSCP: P(charged quirk reaches the end of the tracker) = 8.8e-5 ... 2.2e-8 (triplet, M = 300 ... 2000 GeV), ~0 for the doublet; surviving triplet cross section 0.081 fb (300), 8.3e-4 (500), 8.6e-6 (750), 2.6e-7 fb (1 TeV).
* Helical-compatible fraction (>= 4 hits): ~1.00 for Lambda <= 30 eV (smeared: 0.93-0.99); at c*tau = 1 (5) m, unsmeared / smeared: 0.92/0.81 (0.76/0.60) at 100 eV, 0.49/0.28 (0.21/0.10) at 300 eV, 0.27/0.006 (0.17/0.002) at 1 keV.

## Recast limits

### ATLAS disappearing track, map-based recast (`atlas_dt_recast.py`, `atlas_dt_limits.py`; `data/massscan/atlas_dt_recast_{first,intended}.csv`, `atlas_dt_limits_{first,intended}.csv`)

Uses the public ATLAS-SUSY-2018-19 (arXiv:2201.02472) HEPData efficiency maps (trigger map in MET and leading-jet pT; tracklet map in eta and R_decay), the model-independent limit sigma_vis < 0.037 fb and the official exclusion contour, as distributed with the llprecasting/recastingCodes repository (Ramos and Lessa; commit d4047c7, `DisappearingTracks/ATLAS-SUSY-2018-19`; the repository is not vendored here). The selection is that of the recast code (MET > 200 GeV, leading jet > 100 GeV, min dphi(jets, MET) > 1, 0.1 < |eta| < 1.9, dR(LLP, jet) > 0.4, smeared tracklet pT > 60 GeV, event efficiency = trigger x [1 - prod(1 - eff_i)]). Differences: generator-level anti-kT 0.4 jets and MET from visible particles with a simple parametrised smearing (no Delphes); the charged quirk decays inside the Geant4 simulation (R_decay, eta, pT taken from the simulated decay); recoil-enriched samples (`massscan/rec_gen.sh`, `rec_sim.sh`: generator pair pT > 180 GeV, 20000 events per mass and channel, normalised to the number generated before the filter, M = 300-1500 GeV, Dirac triplet only, Lambda = 30, 100, 300, 1000 eV). **String effect**: the transverse bending angle accumulated by the quirk at r < 125 mm is compared with the magnetic expectation in the simulation (B = 3.8 T); the string-induced part is converted to an equivalent shift of q/pT at the ATLAS field (2 T) and added to the tracklet q/pT smearing (`ideal_helix` columns: shift set to zero). `first` / `intended` are two readings of the pT-bin choice of the recast code's smearing table (the code takes the first matching bin; `intended` takes the highest bin below the track pT); they bracket the systematic.

* Validation: the same chain with the string switched off and wino = triplet/2 gives a limit of 599 (`first`) / 631 (`intended`) GeV at c*tau = 56 mm (tau = 0.19 ns), against 649 GeV on the official contour at this lifetime: the proxy underestimates sigma_vis at the official limit by a factor 1/f = 1.8 / 1.3.
* Dirac triplet mass limit (raw proxy / calibrated to the official wino limit, i.e. sigma_vis / f): Lambda = 30 eV 673-699 / 730-735 GeV; 100 eV 635-662 / 697-708 GeV; 300 eV 517-522 / 557-584 GeV; 1 keV: no exclusion at M >= 300 GeV (the string-induced bias removes the tracklet efficiency; with the string switched off the same sample would give 476-494 / 528-534 GeV). The raw proxy is conservative (f < 1); the calibrated numbers force it to reproduce the official wino limit and are the better central estimate, with the caveat that a single mass-independent factor is assumed. MC statistical error on the crossing ~ +-15 GeV; theory and PDF uncertainty of the NLO+NLL normalisation are not included.
* The earlier flat-upper-limit estimate (750-765 GeV ATLAS-calibrated) is superseded by these numbers; it did not include the mass dependence of the efficiency, the trigger or the string bias.

### ATLAS heavy stable charged particles (`atlas_hscp_recast.py` -> `data/massscan/atlas_hscp_recast.csv`)

Uses the HEPData cross-section limit curves (observed and expected, 1, 4, 10, 30 ns and stable, 400 GeV-1.6 TeV) and the signal cross sections of ATLAS-SUSY-2018-42 (arXiv:2205.06013, 139 fb^-1; recastingCodes `HSCPs/ATLAS-SUSY-2018-42`). A Dirac triplet with Lambda <~ 100 eV is taken to have twice the wino rate (SUSY-WG NLO+NLL, same as the ATLAS signal) and the same acceptance. Reproducing the published wino mass limits with the same crossing procedure (615/956/1026/1046/1048 GeV against the published 624/962/1037/1064/1069 GeV for 1/4/10/30 ns/stable) validates the method to 1-2%. Dirac-triplet limits (observed / expected): 1 ns 758 / 742 GeV; 4 ns 1056 / 1172; 10 ns 1127 / 1283; 30 ns 1150 / 1326; stable 1146 / 1324. These apply only if the charged state is long-lived (c*tau >~ 30 cm, i.e. Delta m far below the loop value). At the predicted c*tau = 54-59 mm (0.19 ns) the ATLAS acceptance (>= 1 charged LLP with r_decay > 500 mm) is 0.001-0.002 against 0.1-0.13 at 1 ns; rescaling the 1 ns limit by this ratio gives sigma_UL >~ 1.6-5.6 pb (M = 300-800 GeV) against sigma_quirk = 0.35-0.013 pb: no sensitivity. The CMS HSCP 2410.09164 and CMS disappearing-track 2004.05153 numbers below remain calibration-based because their HEPData tables could not be retrieved in this environment.

### CMS calibration-based estimate (`recast_limits.py` -> `data/massscan/recast_limits.csv`, older)

The published exclusion of a wino is used as a calibration point and a flat upper limit is applied to the Dirac triplet: a mass is excluded if sigma_triplet x eps_rel(Lambda, M) >= sigma_wino-eq(M_cal), eps_rel = terminating-track rate relative to Lambda = 30 eV, sigma_wino-eq = sigma_triplet/2, own LO cross sections on both sides (K cancels). Calibration points: CMS 2004.05153, wino 474 GeV at 0.2 ns (884 GeV at 3 ns); CMS HSCP 2410.09164, DY tau' (Q = 1e) excluded below 1.02 TeV (101 fb^-1). Dirac triplet: ~550-560 GeV (CMS calibration) for Lambda = 30-100 eV, ~535 GeV at 300 eV; 1 keV: no sensitivity. Doublet: none (c*tau ~ 7-10 mm, decays inside the pixel detector); in this approximation the HSCP plateau (0.066 fb) excludes nothing. These are approximate (flat UL, no mass-dependent efficiency, no track-quality criteria, no lifetime reweighting); +-15 GeV MC noise.

## Production normalisation (`wino_pythia_check.py`, `kfactor_check.py`)

* Independent LO check: Pythia's own SUSY processes (pure-wino SLHA spectrum, SUSY:qqbar2chi+chi-, qqbar2chi+-chi0, same PDF, 13 TeV) give sigma(chi+chi-) = 19.9 fb and sigma(chi+chi0 + chi-chi0) = 41.2 fb at 500 GeV. My per-wino totals (sigma_gen x <w> for DY, kappa_W^2 sigma_gen for W*) are 0.96 (DY) and 0.95 (W*) of those at 500 GeV, 0.96/0.99 at 300 GeV, 0.89/0.89 at 1-2 TeV (`data/massscan/kfactor_check.csv`). The DY/W* ratio of the SUSY processes is 0.48 at 500 GeV, as in my samples.
* Public NLO+NLL wino tables (LHC SUSY Cross Section WG, 13 TeV, as bundled with the ATLAS-SUSY-2018-19 recast (copied from llprecasting/recastingCodes commit d4047c7; check that repository's licence before redistributing): `data/massscan/xsec_13TeV_pp2chgchg_fb.csv`, `..._pp2chgn1_fb.csv`) give the mass-dependent K-factors, `kfactor_check.py` -> `data/massscan/kfactor_check.csv` (`K_DY_own`, `K_W_own`: public NLO+NLL / own LO per wino). The table reproduces the one point verified earlier (ATLAS 2106.01676, 3.42 fb for chi1+- chi2 0 at 850 GeV: table 3.42 fb).

| M (GeV) | 300 | 500 | 750 | 1000 | 1500 | 2000 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K_DY (own LO -> NLO+NLL) | 1.15 | 1.15 | 1.26 | 1.36 | 1.51 | 1.65 |
| K_W | 1.13 | 1.18 | 1.25 | 1.31 | 1.27 | 0.88 |

  The 2 TeV W value (< 1) comes from a Pythia W* sample that is statistically thin and from the PDF-set difference (internal set 20 vs the PDFs of the tables) at large x; use K only up to 1.5 TeV. A single K ~ 1.25 therefore over-states the 300-500 GeV rate by ~8% and under-states DY at >= 1.5 TeV by 20-30%. Tables in `data/` stay at LO except the recast outputs, which are NLO+NLL-scaled per mass and channel. The public tables carry their own scale and PDF uncertainties (~10% at 1 TeV), not propagated.

## Caveats

* Leading order for the quirk samples; the W* and DY samples are Pythia templates (process 3021 with a vector-like W'; gamma*/Z with chiral 4th-generation lepton couplings re-weighted analytically). The re-weighting reproduces Pythia's SUSY wino processes to 4-11%; normalisation is then scaled by the public NLO+NLL tables (K above). Pythia internal PDF set 20 instead of LHAPDF NNPDF3.1.
* Detectors: CMS-like (the card is a reconstruction of public dimensions) and an ITk-like variant whose layer radii and field are checked against arXiv:2412.15090 but whose module geometry/material are generic. Hits are sensitive-volume visits, not reconstructed tracks; the smeared helix fraction is a circle-fit proxy.
* ATLAS disappearing-track recast: generator-level jets and MET with parametrised smearing (no Delphes, no pile-up); trigger via the ATLAS public map (not a trigger simulation); the string effect is a model (q/pT bias from the bending angle at r < 125 mm, no pattern-recognition or fake-rate treatment); the proxy underestimates the official wino limit by 1.3-1.8 in sigma_vis, so raw and calibrated limits are both quoted; only the Dirac triplet is analysed (doublet decays inside the pixel detector, where the map is zero); lifetime is the predicted one (no lifetime reweighting). Recoil-enriched sampling uses a generator pair pT > 180 GeV pre-filter: in the earlier unfiltered samples (M = 300, 500, 1000 GeV, DY and W*, 1840-1970 events each) no event with generator-level pair pT < 180 GeV passes MET > 200 GeV and leading jet > 100 GeV (120-242 events pass per sample), so the filter is lossless at generator level; the resolution smearing can move a few events across the 200 GeV threshold, which is not quantified.
* HSCP recast: acceptance ratios and same-kinematics assumption only; no efficiency map is public for HSCP, so the sub-ns lifetime estimate is indicative. CMS HEPData tables could not be retrieved here (not fetchable from the sandbox; the hepdata.net web pages sit behind a bot check, which was not bypassed); CMS numbers remain calibration-based.
* The per-event agreement of the handover test is limited by step discretisation (see `../quirkdecay/README.md`).
* The patched transport kills a quirk whose forced step ends outside the world; before the fix, Lambda = 10 and 30 eV, c*tau = 1 m runs segfaulted at event 530 of the 500 GeV DY sample. The underlying tiny hIoni step is not identified.
* Not done: CMS limit curves from HEPData; two-loop splitting; a full reconstruction-level terminating-track efficiency; ATLAS monojet recast.
